Hear your phone through your Mac's headphones.

AirPods Max can only hold one connection at a time. Instead of unpairing them from your Mac, this brings the phone's audio across.

What happens when…

You never start this, stop it, or switch it over. Pick a situation and see what the app does about it on its own.

Streaming

Audio starts by itself

The app notices the headphones appear and begins within a second. You don't click anything, and the window doesn't need to be open.

Polls the output device twice a second

Where the 191 milliseconds go

Every figure here was measured on real hardware. Most of the delay is not ours to fix.

Capture buffer15 ms
Output buffer5 ms
Bluetooth — fixed170.7 ms

The last row happens inside the headphones. It is 89% of the wired budget and no setting on either machine changes it — everything this software controls is the other twenty milliseconds. Over Wi‑Fi the buffer grows to 200 ms and the total reaches 376 ms, which is fine for music and noticeable on video.
